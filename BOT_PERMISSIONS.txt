Required permissions for the Discord Bot:

---

## [1] BOT PERMISSIONS (OAuth2 → URL Generator)

## REQUIRED:

[✔] View Channels           — Allows the bot to view channels
[✔] Read Message History    — Allows the bot to read previous messages

## NOT REQUIRED:

[✘] Send Messages
[✘] Manage Messages
[✘] Administrator
[✘] Any other permissions

---

## [2] PRIVILEGED GATEWAY INTENTS (Bot Settings)

Where to find it:
Discord Developer Portal
→ Your Application
→ Click "Bot" on the left
→ Scroll down to "Privileged Gateway Intents"

## REQUIRED:

[✔] Message Content Intent — Allows the bot to read message content

After enabling it, click "Save Changes".

---

## [3] HOW TO ADD THE BOT TO YOUR SERVER

Step 1: Open Discord Developer Portal
https://discord.com/developers/applications

Step 2: Select your Application
→ OAuth2 → URL Generator

Step 3: Under SCOPES, select only:
[✔] bot

Step 4: Under BOT PERMISSIONS, select:
[✔] View Channels
[✔] Read Message History

Step 5: Copy the Generated URL

Step 6: Open the URL in your browser
→ Select your Discord Server
→ Click "Authorize"

The bot will now be added to your server.

---

## SUMMARY

Bot Permissions : View Channels + Read Message History
Gateway Intent  : Message Content Intent (ON)

No other permissions are required.
This tool is read-only and does not send, edit, or delete messages.
